# Day 1 Guidance V1

## Product priorities

1. Guide the player clearly.
2. Keep the playfield visually comfortable.
3. Strengthen gameplay after the player understands the core flow.

## Implemented in this step

- Replace the text-heavy Day 1 briefing with a quick visual route through the five actions.
- Replace the persistent five-row checklist with a compact current-step coach card.
- Show only the current action, the next action, and overall step progress during play.
- Remove inaccurate instructions about automatic walking and shelf timers from the guided level.
- Make the load step visibly show an empty cart, the case beside it, and a highlighted drop area.
- Keep the guided level free of a shift countdown.

## Deferred

- New crisis events and parallel store jobs.
- Deeper scoring and replay decisions.
- Final art and animation polish beyond the guidance surfaces.
